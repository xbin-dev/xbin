01 · Default-deny

Closed until granted.

Nothing inside a sandbox reaches any of these until someone grants it. Every approved grant is written to a plain, versioned file.

  1. 01

    The network

    Outbound network is default-deny. Each destination is approved on its own.

  2. 02

    Other apps

    Calls between apps pass through xbin, which checks who is calling and what they have been granted.

  3. 03

    GPUs

    A sandbox reaches no GPU until one is granted to it.

  4. 04

    Stored data

    A sandbox reaches no stored data until it is granted. Each app keeps its secrets in its own encrypted vault.

02 · Sandboxes

Every app and every agent runs in its own sandbox.

That includes the built-in agent and the coding agents: Claude Code, Codex, Gemini CLI and opencode.

A person signs a coding agent in once, and that sign-in is reused.

03 · Grants

Every grant is a line in a file.

A grant is an approved permission for an app to reach something outside itself: a network destination, another app, a GPU or stored data.

Approved grants are kept in a plain, versioned file. You can read it, and you can diff two versions to see exactly what an approval changed.

04 · Identity and access

xbin knows who is calling.

People. People sign in with single sign-on through OIDC or GitHub. They belong to organisations and hold roles.

Apps. Each app has its own identity and its own grants. Every call passes through xbin, which checks the caller and what it has been granted, so an app never authenticates its callers itself.

Coding agents. A person signs a coding agent in once, and the sign-in is reused.

Phones. The iOS app signs in with the iPhone’s secure hardware.

05 · Oversight

A record, and a read-only view.

xbin keeps an audit log.

View-as-user lets an admin see exactly what one person sees. The view is read-only.

06 · Per-person data

A person’s copy is theirs.

An app can run one copy for each person. Other people cannot read it, and admins do not open it.

A person’s key-value data is protected with envelope encryption. Their files, SQLite databases and blob storage sit in an encrypted volume of their own.

07 · Secrets, backups and limits

Sealed, encrypted, limited.

  • Secrets

    Each app keeps its secrets in its own encrypted vault.

  • Backups

    Backups are sealed and encrypted.

  • Limits

    Each app runs within its own resource limits. Outbound network follows a policy: default-deny, approved per destination.
