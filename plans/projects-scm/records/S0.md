## Decision text

Projects-scm §17.3 drafts the program's decision. S0's own choices are
§18's lines, chiefly: "run as root" is not added to the sandbox-manager
contract (ACP's `terminal/create` has no user field, sdk/acp/types.go:366;
sudo in VM sandboxes is the `sbx/dev-root` track); registration points
instead of edits to `routes.go`/`migrate.go`; CI as a section of the one
right dock (the D147 board becomes its host) rather than a second dock;
progress events opt-in per subscription and one delivery per consumer.
